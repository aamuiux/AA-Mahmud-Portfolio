Core Principles

Three pillars of high-impact product design.

01

User-Validated Discovery & ROI

Every design decision starts with real user behavior and commercial targets. By combining stakeholder interviews, qualitative research, and heuristic audits, I identify high-friction bottlenecks and anchor design to measurable retention and conversion metrics.

02

Scalable Design Systems & Tokens

Enterprise software requires unified governance. I build token-driven Figma libraries, accessible components (WCAG AA), and clear documentation that scale across 20+ squads — cutting front-end regression tickets and accelerating sprint velocity by 60%+.

03

AI-Native UX & High-Density Clarity

Modern intelligent platforms demand thoughtful human-in-the-loop workflows. I replace ambiguous prompt boxes with granular steering dials, progressive disclosure, and real-time feedback states that make complex AI feel predictable and empowering.

Design Framework

A proven process for repeatable product success.

  1. 01

    Discovery, User Research & Audits

    Conducting stakeholder interviews, user journey mapping, competitive benchmarking, and heuristic friction audits to align product requirements with core business OKRs and customer pain points.

  2. 02

    Information Architecture & Wireframing

    Mapping complex user mental models, task flows, and low-fidelity interactive wireframes to validate usability hypotheses rapidly before committing engineering or design resources.

  3. 03

    UI Craft, Design Systems & Usability Testing

    Designing pixel-perfect, accessible (WCAG AA) interfaces in Figma, building modular design token architectures, and running moderated user testing to iterate with empirical data.

  4. 04

    Developer Handoff, QA & Telemetry Iteration

    Delivering comprehensive Figma tokens, state matrices (empty, error, loading, edge cases), and responsive specs. Partnering with engineers through QA and monitoring post-launch telemetry to drive compounding retention.

Recruiter & Client FAQ

Common questions & working models.

For 0-to-1 initiatives, I focus on rapid discovery, user interviews, low-fidelity wireframes, and fast prototype validation to prove product-market fit before scaling. For legacy enterprise software, I perform detailed heuristic audits, identify high-friction bottlenecks, and implement modular design systems that modernize usability without alienating existing power users.

Design must drive measurable business outcomes. I measure success through concrete product metrics: onboarding completion rates, task execution velocity, churn reduction, feature adoption, and Net Promoter Score (NPS). For design system projects, ROI is measured in accelerated engineering velocity and reduced bug remediation hours.

I treat cross-functional collaboration as fundamental. By engaging engineers during early discovery, documenting comprehensive component edge cases (empty states, errors, latency, focus states), and using structured Figma design tokens, we eliminate handoff friction and build shared product ownership.

I am available for Lead UI/UX Designer full-time positions, Fractional Design Leadership (10–20 hours/week) for venture-backed startups, and targeted 4–8 week Design Sprints focused on product discovery, redesigns, or design systems.
